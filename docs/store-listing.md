### Long description (Play + App Store)

Dahr is a wedding marketplace for Libya. Couples discover approved vendors, save favorites, and send a booking request. Vendors list a business, upload photos, manage dates, and answer from an inbox. Arabic is the default (RTL); English is a tap away.

What you can do

• Discover venues, photography, catering, dresses, beauty, music, cars, decor, and more in Tripoli and Benghazi
• Open a vendor page for photos, price range in LYD, and reviews
• Request a booking for a free date and add a message
• Message the vendor on WhatsApp (opens WhatsApp; Dahr does not store those chats)
• Vendors: dashboard, inbox, calendar, and photo manager after you sign in
• Sign in with Email OTP only — we email a one-time code. No passwords and no in-app phone SMS login
• Delete your account anytime in Profile → Delete account

What Dahr does not do

• No in-app card payments, Apple IAP, or Google Play Billing
• You pay the vendor off-platform (often via WhatsApp or in person), in LYD
• After a vendor accepts a quote, the couple pays Dahr a 10% platform fee by bank transfer — that is not charged as an in-app purchase. The rest of the quote is still settled with the vendor off-platform

Guest browse of Discover works without an account. Booking, favorites, and vendor tools require sign-in.
